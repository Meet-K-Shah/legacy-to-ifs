# Master-Data Governance - Roles & RACI

| Domain | Data Owner (accountable) | Data Steward (responsible) | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Item / Part | VP Supply Chain | Item Data Steward (Supply Chain) | Engineering, Quality, Finance (costing) | Procurement, Operations |
| BOM / Product structure | VP Engineering | Engineering Data Steward | Manufacturing, Supply Chain | Estimating |
| Customer (bill-to / ship-to) | VP Sales Operations | Customer Master Steward (AR) | Finance, Credit | Sales, Project Mgmt |
| Vendor | VP Procurement | Vendor Master Steward (AP) | Finance, Quality (approved vendor list), Tax | Procurement |
| Chart of accounts / GL | Controller | Finance Systems Analyst | Business-unit finance leads | Executive team |
| Inventory & open orders (migration only) | Director Operations | Inventory Control Lead | Finance | Planners |

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Data & Analytics (ERP data lead)** | Owns the data workstream plan, quality KPIs, pipelines, issue log, decision log, cutover checklist; facilitates cross-BU decisions; escalation point for master-data questions |
| **Data Owner** | Accepts data standards, signs off cutover data, decides cross-BU convergence |
| **Data Steward** | Day-to-day create/change/retire approvals, resolves the review queue, works defect backlog to closure |
| **Finance sign-off** | Owns dollar parity, GL tie-out, adjusting journals |
